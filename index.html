Who

I'm an AI builder and Pursuit Fellow in New York, with a background in marketing, education, and human-centered design.

Before AI, I spent years as a tutor — learning to read a person quickly and meet them exactly where they are. That instinct is the whole point of my work now: tools that adapt to who someone is, especially when the stakes are high and existing technology has left them behind. I'm training in a selective AI-Native program backed by Amazon and Google, and building in public.
